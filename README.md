# FoodLink 

> A digital platform connecting local food businesses with charities to eliminate food waste and support communities.

---

##  Project Overview
**Course:** Software Engineering Graduation Project  
**Title:** FoodLink  

FoodLink is a simple web platform designed to facilitate real-time food donation between local businesses (restaurants, bakeries, supermarkets) and charitable organizations.

---

##  Problem & Solution

* **The Problem:** Local businesses throw away fresh unsold food daily while charities struggle to find enough food for people in need.
* **The Solution:** A centralized app where businesses can quickly post surplus food, and charities can reserve and pick it up efficiently.

---

##  Target Users

1. **Food Donors:** Business owners posting leftover food listings.
2. **Charities:** Shelters and food banks searching and reserving donations.
3. **Administrator:** System manager verifying accounts and monitoring activities.

---

##  Main Features (MVP)

* User registration & account verification.
* Easy food listing creation (quantity, pickup times, address).
* Searchable dashboard with real-time donation availability.
* Email / System notifications for local charities.
* Pickup confirmation receipt.
* Admin panel to oversee platform statistics.

---

##  Out of Scope (Version 1)

* In-app delivery or courier dispatch.
* Online payment integration.
* AI-based food expiration/supply prediction.
* fully multilingual application (initially one language only).
